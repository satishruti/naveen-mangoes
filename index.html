Our Story

From Southeast Asia's
Finest Orchards
to Karnataka's Soil

Naveen Mangoes is a pioneering organic fruit farm located on NH-50 Gulbarga-Bijapur Highway, Vijayapura 586127, Karnataka. We cultivate premium Thailand and Japan variety mangoes — among the most prized exotic varieties in the world — right here in India.

Our farm uses modern drip irrigation and sustainable farming methods. Every mango is allowed to ripen naturally on the tree — no calcium carbide, no ethylene gas, no shortcuts. The result is unmatched flavour, nutrition, and fragrance.

Our work has been recognised in the Kannada press and by government officials — a testament to the quality and innovation this farm brings to Karnataka's agriculture.

Our Farming Philosophy

Farming the Way
Nature Intended

At Naveen Mangoes, every decision on the farm starts with one question: what does nature want? Our commitment to natural, chemical-free farming is what makes the difference in every bite.

01

Drip Irrigation System

Our modern drip irrigation delivers water precisely to the root zone of each tree, reducing water waste and ensuring consistent fruit development throughout the year.

02

Tree-Ripening Only — No Chemicals

Every fruit stays on the tree until full natural maturity. Absolutely no calcium carbide, no ethylene gas chambers, no artificial shortcuts. Ever.

03

Exotic Variety Cultivation

We grow Thailand and Japan mango varieties — brought from Southeast Asia and carefully cultivated to thrive in Karnataka's climate and rich black cotton soil.

04

Hand-Picked & Farm Direct

Each fruit is hand-picked at peak natural ripeness, handled gently, and delivered directly from our farm to you — no long cold-chain delays, no middlemen.
