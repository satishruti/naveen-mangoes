Our Story

From Thailand's Orchards
to Karnataka's Heartland

Naveen Mangoes is a pioneering farm on NH-50, Gulbarga–Bijapur Highway, Vijayapura 586127 — bringing the most prized Thailand variety mango to Indian soil, grown the pure way.

Our farm uses modern drip irrigation and chemical-free farming. Featured in Kannada media and officially recognised by Vijayapura government officials for pioneering Thailand mango cultivation in this region.

Every mango is tree-ripened — no calcium carbide, no ethylene gas. Just Karnataka sun, patience, and passion for quality.

Our Farming Philosophy

How We Grow
the Perfect Mango

At Naveen Mangoes every decision comes back to one principle: let nature lead. Our farm on NH-50 uses modern drip irrigation in service of pure, chemical-free farming.

01

Drip Irrigation System

Precision drip lines deliver exactly the right water to every tree root — conserving water while maximising fruit quality and sweetness.

02

Tree-Ripening Only

Zero calcium carbide, zero ethylene chambers. Every fruit stays on the tree until it naturally signals it's ready — that's when we pick.

03

Natural Soil Nutrition

Organic composts and natural inputs keep our black cotton Vijayapura soil fertile — no synthetic fertilisers that compromise taste.

04

Hand-Picked & Farm-Packed

Every mango is hand-selected at peak ripeness, carefully packed at our facility, and dispatched same-day for maximum freshness.
