Our Story

From Southeast Asia's
Finest Orchards
to Karnataka's Soil

Naveen Mangoes is a pioneering organic fruit farm located on NH-50 Gulbarga-Bijapur Highway, Vijayapura 586127, Karnataka. We cultivate premium Thailand and Japan variety mangoes — among the most prized exotic varieties in the world — right here in India.

Our farm uses modern drip irrigation and sustainable, chemical-free farming methods. Every mango is allowed to ripen naturally on the tree — no calcium carbide, no artificial shortcuts. The result is unmatched flavour, full nutrition, and genuine farm-to-home freshness.

Our pioneering work has been featured in Kannada media and recognised by government officials — making us one of the first farms in the Vijayapura region to commercially supply exotic Thailand and Japan mango varieties.

Our Farming Philosophy

Farming the Way
Nature Intended

At Naveen Mangoes, every decision on the farm starts with one question: what does nature want? Our commitment to natural, chemical-free farming is what makes the difference in every bite.

01

Modern Drip Irrigation

Our precision drip irrigation delivers water directly to the root zone of each tree — reducing waste and ensuring consistent fruit development year-round, even in dry months.

02

Tree-Ripening — Zero Chemicals

Every fruit stays on the tree until full natural maturity. Absolutely no calcium carbide, no ethylene gas, no artificial shortcuts — ever. Your family deserves only the real thing.

03

Exotic Variety Cultivation

We grow Thailand and Japan mango varieties — brought from Southeast Asia and carefully cultivated to thrive in Vijayapura's climate and Karnataka's rich black cotton soil.

04

Hand-Picked & Farm Direct

Each fruit is hand-picked at peak natural ripeness, handled gently, and dispatched directly from our farm to you — no cold-chain delays, no middlemen, maximum freshness.
