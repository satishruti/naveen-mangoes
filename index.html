Our Story

From Thailand's Orchards
to Karnataka's Soil

Naveen Mangoes began with a singular obsession: bringing the most prized mango variety in Southeast Asia and growing it right here in India — without compromise.

Our farm sits approximately 25 km from Vijayapura (Bijapur), Karnataka, where the climate and rich black cotton soil create perfect conditions for these exotic Thailand variety mangoes to flourish.

Every mango that leaves our farm is naturally ripened on the tree — no calcium carbide, no ethylene gas, no shortcuts. Just patience, sunshine, and the same love farmers have shown for generations.

Our Farming Philosophy

Farming the Way
Nature Intended

At Naveen Mangoes, we believe the best mango is one the tree decided to ripen — not a chemical plant. Every decision we make on the farm starts with one question: what does nature want?

01

Natural Soil Care

We maintain soil health using organic composts and natural inputs, keeping the land fertile without synthetic fertilisers or chemicals.

02

Tree-Ripening Only

Every fruit stays on the tree until full natural maturity — no calcium carbide, no ethylene chambers, no artificial shortcuts.

03

Hand-Picked with Care

Our team hand-picks each fruit at peak ripeness, handling gently to avoid bruising and preserve the farm-fresh quality.

04

Direct to Your Door

From farm to hands — no long cold-chain delays, no middlemen. Just pure freshness delivered directly to you.
