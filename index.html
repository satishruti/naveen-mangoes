Our Story

From Thailand's Orchards
to Karnataka's Soil

Naveen Mangoes is a pioneering farm on NH-50, Gulbarga–Bijapur Highway, Vijayapura 586127. We grow the world's most prized Thailand variety mango in Indian soil — completely naturally.

Featured in Kannada media. Recognised by Vijayapura government officials. One of the first farms in this region to commercially cultivate Thailand mangoes at scale with year-round production.

Every mango is tree-ripened — no calcium carbide, no ethylene gas. Just Karnataka sun, drip irrigation, and obsessive care for quality.

Our Farming

How We Grow
the Perfect Mango

At Naveen Mangoes every decision starts with one principle: let nature lead. Our farm uses modern drip irrigation in service of pure, chemical-free growing.

01

Drip Irrigation System

Precision drip lines to every tree root — conserving water while building sweeter, denser fruit.

02

Tree-Ripening Only

Zero calcium carbide. Zero ethylene. Every fruit waits on the tree until it naturally ripens.

03

Natural Soil Care

Vijayapura's black cotton soil + organic composts — no synthetic fertilisers that harm taste.

04

Hand-Picked & Packed

Every mango hand-selected, carefully packed at our NH-50 facility, dispatched same-day.
